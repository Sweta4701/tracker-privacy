Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2025

This Privacy Policy explains how Suraj Informatics Pvt. Ltd. (SIPL) collects, uses, and protects your information when you use the Tracker app — an internal workforce management platform for employees and managers to manage timesheets, activities, leave, and work exceptions.
👥

1. Who Uses This App

Tracker is exclusively for authorized SIPL employees and managers. It supports two user roles:

  • 👷 Employees
  • 🧑‍💼 Managers / Supervisors
This app is not available to the general public. Only verified SIPL personnel with valid credentials can access it.
📋

2. Information We Collect

  • Login credentials — Employee ID and password used to authenticate.
  • Timesheet data — Daily/weekly work hours you fill and submit.
  • Activity records — Tasks and project activities created or updated.
  • Leave requests — Leave type, dates, and approval status.
  • Exception entries — Attendance discrepancies raised or reverted.
  • Location data — Device GPS may be accessed for attendance verification.
  • Device info — Device model and OS version for technical support.
⚙️

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To authenticate employees and grant access to the app.
  • To record, manage, and report daily work activities and timesheets.
  • To process leave applications and notify employees of approval status.
  • To allow managers to allocate employees to projects and tasks.
  • To handle attendance exceptions and maintain accurate records.
  • To generate workforce reports for management visibility.
🔒

4. Data Sharing

Your data is shared only within SIPL's internal systems — between employees, their reporting managers, and authorized HR/admin personnel — strictly for workforce management purposes.

🚫 We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with any third party for advertising or commercial purposes.
🛡️

5. Data Security

  • All data is transmitted over HTTPS (encrypted connection).
  • Data is stored securely on SIPL's internal servers.
  • Access is restricted to authorized personnel only.
  • Login sessions are managed with secure token-based authentication.
✋

6. Your Rights

As an employee using Tracker, you have the right to:

  • View your submitted timesheets, activities, and leave records within the app.
  • Raise exceptions to correct any inaccurate attendance data.
  • Request data correction or deletion by contacting your IT/admin team.
📬
